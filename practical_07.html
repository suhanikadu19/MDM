Interview FAQ

What questions are asked in an interview? +

Common Interview Questions:

  • Tell me about yourself.

    • When asked to tell me about myself, I typically start by providing a brief overview of my professional background and key achievements. I then highlight my relevant skills and experiences that align with the position I'm applying for.

  • What are your strengths?
  • What are your weaknesses?
  • Why should we hire you?
  • Where do you see yourself in 5 years?
